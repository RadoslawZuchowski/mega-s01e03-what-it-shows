These are separate causes and have separate tickets: #1, parser (379 reports) and #2, booking-v3 hint (33 reports). Both tickets are closed to match the engineering update. Lessons

  • Deduplicate export rows by report ID before counting; repeated rows are not new reports.
  • Group by tenant locale and form behaviour before opening tickets. Intake tags and matching subjects do not establish that reports share a cause.
